# Facebook

I have one kidney.

About six months after kidney-cancer surgery, I learned that someone I love is living with kidney failure and goes to dialysis three times each week. I wish I could give the kidney he may need. I cannot.

That limitation led me to learn more about organ, eye, and tissue donation—and about the ways we can still help when we cannot give an organ: reliable information, a ride, a meal, a visit, blood donation, or simply staying connected.

I wrote a new Cornerstone called **The Gift I Cannot Give**. It uses trusted federal and Texas sources and points readers toward official next steps.

Perhaps today, you can leave one light on for someone.

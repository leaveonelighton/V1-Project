## Article title
The Gift I Cannot Give

## Launch post
I have one kidney. That is not a metaphor.

About six months after surgery for kidney cancer, I learned that someone I love is living with kidney failure and receives dialysis three times each week. I cannot give a kidney. I have one kidney left, and it needs to stay with me.

So I began learning what remains possible: donation after death, living kidney donation, paired exchange, and the practical support people can offer even when they cannot donate.

This new Leave One Light On Cornerstone is personal, carefully sourced, and written without pressure.

Perhaps today, you can leave one light on for someone.

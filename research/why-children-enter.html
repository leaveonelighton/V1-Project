Why Children Enter Foster Care

A plain-language evidence guide from Leave One Light On.

Reviewed July 2026 · U.S. scope

What is the issue?

Children usually come to the attention of child welfare after concerns about abuse or neglect. A report may be screened out, investigated, or handled through an alternative response. Out-of-home care is considered when safety cannot be maintained at home.

Why does it matter?

Simple explanations can stigmatize parents, children, or communities. Poverty can destabilize families, but poverty alone is not the same as neglect. Prevention and concrete support may help families remain safely together.

What does current evidence say?

The 2025 federal factsheet on how the child welfare system works explains that decisions depend on state law, immediate safety, future risk, court involvement, available services, and the family’s circumstances. It reports that neglect accounts for a large share of substantiated maltreatment while explicitly cautioning that poverty does not inherently equal neglect.

What is one meaningful step I can take today?

Turn learning into one practical action.

Support a local family-resource center, food bank, housing service, recovery program, or kinship navigator. Prevention is also child welfare.

  • Do not apply population-level findings to an individual.

  • Data definitions and reporting years vary.
  • People with lived experience are more than an outcome measure.

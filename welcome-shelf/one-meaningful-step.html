Take one step

One Meaningful Step Planner

Notice the need. Choose compassion. Take one meaningful step.

This worksheet is for moments when you care about something but are not sure what to do next. Keep the step small enough to complete and responsible enough to be useful.

Back to Welcome Shelf

1. What did I notice?

Describe the need, barrier, question, or opportunity without trying to solve it yet.

2. Who is affected?

Keep the person in view. Who is closest to the need, and whose perspective may be missing?

3. What do I know - and what do I still need to learn?

What I know

What I need to learn

4. What is one responsible next step?

Choose one action you can realistically take. It may be learning, asking, showing up, giving time, connecting with a qualified organization, or offering practical help.

Afterward

Ask: Did this help? What did I learn? Is there another responsible step - or is it time to hand the need to someone better equipped?

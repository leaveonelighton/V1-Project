Before the conversation

  • Choose a facilitator and a realistic amount of time.
  • Make participation voluntary.
  • Keep the conversation focused on practical service rather than pressure or disclosure.
  • Use qualified organizations when specialized support, screening, training, or safeguarding is required.

Team invitation menu

Offer several forms of participation so that money, health, schedule, mobility, and caregiving responsibilities do not become tests of commitment.

Give time

Example

A voluntary service shift or mentoring orientation

Boundary

Use qualified partners and realistic schedules.

Give skill

Example

Resume help, translation, repair, logistics, design

Boundary

Confirm the partner actually needs the skill.

Give practical support

Example

Requested supplies or a meal train

Boundary

Use a current list; do not guess needs.

Give life

Example

Blood donation information or a team appointment

Boundary

Participation and medical eligibility are private.

Give opportunity

Example

Fair interview access, job shadowing, or training

Boundary

Do not promise employment or exploit stories.

Give attention

Example

Learn, listen, write encouragement, share a resource

Boundary

Protect privacy and avoid savior language.

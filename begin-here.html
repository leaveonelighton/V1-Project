Begin Here

Welcome. You do not need all the answers to begin.

Leave One Light On is a place to learn, reflect, find trustworthy resources, and discover practical ways to strengthen the life of another person with dignity and care.

What this is

Every person has been entrusted with something.

For some, that may be time. For others, knowledge, experience, professional skills, encouragement, prayer, financial resources, hospitality, or simply the willingness to remain present.

Leave One Light On helps people recognize what is already within reach and consider one responsible next step. No one is expected to do everything. No single kind of participation is presented as morally superior to another.

DignityPeople are never projects, statistics, or proof of our goodness.
MercyWe move toward need without exploiting pain.
StewardshipWe ask what has already been entrusted to us.
Practical compassionCare becomes useful through responsible action.

A simple way to use this site

Notice. Understand. Choose. Act.

  1. Notice what brought you here. A story, a need, a question, or a desire to serve is enough.
  2. Understand before acting. Use the research and resources when the subject deserves careful learning.
  3. Choose what fits your capacity. Health, time, finances, experience, and circumstances differ from person to person.
  4. Take one meaningful next step. Learn more, make a call, mentor, give blood, pray, encourage, share a resource, or support the work in a way that is right for you.

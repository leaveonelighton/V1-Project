What is Leave One Light On?

We explore what helps people move forward when learning, belonging, opportunity, or support is missing.

That can mean foster care, reading and learning barriers, education, mentorship, technology and work, health, or second chances. Different subjects; the same commitment to see the person clearly and learn responsibly.

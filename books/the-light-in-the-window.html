The Light in the Window

Margaret “Peg” Gorman and Theodore “Ted” Johnson

The story that began the movement

At thirteen, Ted arrived at Cedar Hill Road looking for a place to belong. Peg and Jerry Gorman opened their home—and kept opening it to young people who needed welcome, stability, and family.

The book preserves the people, memories, grief, humor, faith, and ordinary acts of compassion that later inspired Leave One Light On.

The Light in the Window tells the story. Leave One Light On invites us to live it.

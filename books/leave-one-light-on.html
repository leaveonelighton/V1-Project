Leave One Light On

by Theodore Johnson

The practical invitation of the movement

Inspired by the true story behind the movement, Leave One Light On invites ordinary people to practice compassion, hospitality, hope, and faithful presence in practical ways.

Theodore Johnson connects the movement’s message to achievable acts of care—ways to see a need, respond with kindness, and be present for one person without believing we must do everything.

The Light in the Window tells the story.
Leave One Light On invites us to live it.

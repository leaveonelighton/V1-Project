Why we exist

About Ted

The first of fifty-seven and steward of the invitation.

Someone left a light on for him.

Ted Johnson arrived at Cedar Hill Road at thirteen. Peg and Jerry Gorman’s welcome became an inheritance he now carries through the books and the Leave One Light On movement.

The movement is not a promise that Ted or this website can solve every need. It is an invitation to learn, connect with qualified organizations, and choose one responsible act of compassion.

Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. So does your privacy.

Effective date: To be assigned upon publication

Our commitment

Leave One Light On respects the dignity and privacy of visitors. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit this site or contact us.

Information you choose to provide

You may send a message, topic, optional name or nickname, and—only if you choose an email, text, or phone reply—contact information. A private website reply does not require an email address or phone number. Please do not send Social Security numbers, financial account information, passwords, medical records, private foster-care case details, or identifying information about children.

How information is used

We use submitted information to respond to requests, review corrections, consider partnership ideas, operate the messaging system, maintain security, and prevent abuse. Sending a message does not subscribe you to newsletters or ongoing updates.

Private messages and response codes

A private website reply uses a code to retrieve responses. Keep your code secure. The system is designed to protect optional contact information and response codes, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Messages are not a crisis service.

Technical information and service providers

Hosting and website systems may process request details and security logs as needed to deliver and protect this site. Third-party hosting, payment, book-shopping and linked services may process information under their own policies. If we introduce additional analytics or tracking tools, we will update this policy to describe them.

Sharing

We do not publish private correspondence without appropriate permission. Information may be handled by providers operating website services or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect people and website security.

Retention

Information is retained as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain security and comply with legal obligations. Specific deletion intervals for private messages are under operational review and will be stated here when confirmed.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional details. For questions or requests regarding information you submitted, email contact@leaveonelighton.org. Requests will be evaluated in light of applicable legal and security obligations.

External sites and children

External websites have their own privacy practices. Do not submit sensitive information about children or private foster-care case records via general contact forms.

Policy changes

Changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date. We may update this policy when website features, providers, or applicable requirements change.

Contact

Write to contact@leaveonelighton.org or visit the Contact page.

Leave One Light On is not a crisis service. Find immediate support resources.

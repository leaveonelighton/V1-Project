Gorman first-person stories — The Other 56

This optional submission path is for people choosing to share their own Gorman experience. It is not a way to identify or locate other former foster children. You may keep your story private, submit for private review only, or expressly consent to website publication after owner moderation.

A name or pseudonym and private email are optional. Public attribution uses only the name or pseudonym you chose; choose Anonymous to keep any supplied name private. Your email is never published. The owner can read your original and optional contact details; these are encrypted in storage. Public output contains only reviewed, consented wording and the approved attribution. No submission is automatically published. Permission does not include merchandise, advertising, newsletters or social reuse.

Save the private receipt to check status or withdraw via Check my story. Withdrawal removes the hosted public story and attribution and prevents republication. Copies already shared elsewhere may remain. If the receipt is lost, contact LOLO from the private email you supplied, if any, to request removal. Requests are verified privately; do not provide identity documents through the story form.

On the active database, pending entries expire after 90 days without updates; declined or withdrawn entries after 30 days. Private originals, optional contact details and moderator notes for published stories are removed after 30 days. Approved public wording and attribution remain until removal. Scheduled cleanup must be verified before intake opens. Backup deletion periods will be confirmed before activation and may differ from active-database retention.

Spam prevention uses a honeypot, timing check and rate limits. Do not include private foster-care case records, private information about children, or identifying details about others. You do not need to share anything to be part of this history.

# AI Project Charter — Leave One Light On

## Purpose

This charter keeps all AI-assisted work aligned with the movement's identity, mission, priorities, standards, and current stage of development.

It is a guardrail, not a straitjacket. It exists to prevent drift, unnecessary complexity, factual confusion, premature technology, and unauthorized changes.

AI may analyze, propose, draft, validate, and organize.

The owner decides.

## Mission

Leave One Light On exists to encourage practical compassion by helping ordinary people discover hope, understanding, trusted resources, and meaningful opportunities to strengthen the lives of others.

## Foundational Principle

> Technology is a tool. Trust is the foundation.

## Brand Hierarchy

Primary public identity:

Leave One Light On

Founding doorway:

Fifty-Seven

Guiding principle:

Practical Compassion

Approved positioning rule:

> Fifty-Seven opens the door. Leave One Light On is where the visitor arrives.

Fifty-Seven remains part of the founding story and cornerstone research. It is not a separate public brand, organization, campaign, or initiative.

Do not create Project 57, Unite 57, or similar public identities without explicit owner approval.

## Website Purpose

The website helps ordinary people discover:

- Hope — reassurance that their lives and actions matter
- Practical help — trusted resources and realistic next steps
- Understanding — clear, contextualized, nonpartisan information
- One meaningful next step — appropriate to the visitor's readiness

## Content Journey

Story → Understanding → Action

Visitors often arrive through story. They gain understanding. They are then offered one realistic and dignified invitation to act.

## Public Architecture — Directional

The long-term directional architecture is:

- Home

This is a conceptual content architecture.

It is not automatic permission to replace the current navigation, rename existing sections, change established URLs, or restructure the website.

Navigation changes require a specific work order and explicit owner approval.

## Build Priorities

Apply these priorities in order:

1. Clarity
2. Trust
3. Content
4. Invitation
5. Relationships
6. Technology

Technology must solve an established need.

Do not recommend or build an application, dashboard, database, animated counter, submission system, authentication system, or complex interactive feature merely because it is technically possible.

## Research Standards

Every factual claim must be:

- Attributable
- Current enough for its stated purpose
- Presented in context
- Supported by an appropriate source
- Clearly distinguished from personal story, interpretation, opinion, or invitation

Prefer authoritative primary sources for factual and time-sensitive claims.

Do not fabricate statistics, quotations, citations, dates, names, organizations, URLs, or research findings.

Clearly distinguish among:

- Personal story
- Historical statement
- Research finding
- Interpretation
- Opinion
- Invitation or call to action

When evidence changes, update the wording and explain the limitation rather than defending outdated language.

Population-level findings must not be treated as predictions about an individual.

## AI Guardrails

AI must not:

- Rename Leave One Light On
- Create a competing public brand
- Create Project 57, Unite 57, or similar standalone brands without owner approval
- Replace or restructure navigation without a specific approved work order
- Change established URLs without an approved redirect plan
- Add applications, dashboards, counters, databases, forms, user accounts, or submission systems prematurely
- Fabricate facts, statistics, sources, quotes, endorsements, partnerships, impact, or results
- Present speculation as an approved decision
- Treat brainstorming as authorization
- Publish sensitive or identifying personal information without approval
- Reduce people to labels, outcomes, statistics, or inspirational material
- Commit, push, merge, publish, deploy, delete, reset, or overwrite work without explicit owner approval

AI must preserve historical accuracy.

AI must protect dignity, privacy, and context.

AI must report uncertainty, limitations, failed checks, inaccessible sources, and incomplete validation honestly.

## Tone Standard

Content should be:

- Warm, not cold or clinical
- Clear, using plain language
- Dignified, never reducing people to statistics or circumstances
- Nonpartisan
- Faith-aware without coercion
- Invitational rather than guilt-driven
- Practical rather than performative
- Hopeful without making unrealistic promises
- Personal where appropriate without exploiting private experiences

Prefer language such as:

- Consider
- Explore
- Learn
- Begin
- One practical step
- What is within reach?

Avoid manipulative urgency, shame, inflated claims, savior language, and promises the movement cannot guarantee.

## Page Test

Every proposed page must accomplish at least one of the following:

- Tell a meaningful story
- Teach something trustworthy
- Offer one realistic next step

Whenever appropriate, a page may accomplish all three without overwhelming the visitor.

If a proposed page accomplishes none of them, it does not belong.

## Content Principles

- Fifty-Seven is the founding doorway, not the destination.
- Leave One Light On is the movement and public identity.
- Practical Compassion is the guiding principle expressed through action.
- The static website is the authoritative content source.
- Social platforms invite people toward the website; they are not the permanent content archive.
- Current navigation remains until separately reviewed and approved.
- Invitation must not become obligation.
- People must not be used as evidence of the movement's goodness.
- Every practical recommendation should respect consent, qualifications, boundaries, and local context.

## Change and Release Process

For website or governance changes:

1. Begin from an approved and clean source branch.
2. Create a clearly named work branch.
3. Apply only the authorized scope.
4. Validate structure, links, accessibility, metadata, responsive behavior, and factual sources as applicable.
5. Return the exact changed-file list and diff summary.
6. Await owner review.
7. Commit only after owner approval.
8. Push or merge only after owner approval.
9. Deploy only after explicit release approval.
10. Verify the live result and record the deployed commit.

No orphan work.

Every file must belong to an approved work order, documented decision, release, or backlog item.

## Success Measures

The movement primarily measures success through:

- Trust earned
- People encouraged
- Useful resources created
- Meaningful next steps taken
- Relationships and partnerships formed
- Dignity protected
- Commitments honored
- Practical compassion sustained over time

Website visits, followers, impressions, clicks, shares, and subscriber counts are useful indicators.

They are not the mission.

# SOMETIMES 57 — permanent LOLO signature collection

Owner-approved direction: November 6, 2026. A lasting expression of Leave One Light On, rooted in the Gorman legacy. It is not a new organization or a seasonal promotion.

## Approved wording and hierarchy

1. Signature: **SOMETIMES 57.** Keep the final period. A stacked SOMETIMES / 57. composition is approved.
2. Origin: *It started with room for one more.*
3. Invitation: **It doesn't have to end there.**
4. Companion book/home message: **A home can change a life. Sometimes 57.**

Keep these roles clear. Use the complete signature on the permanent story section and in product descriptions. Merchandise may use the first two lines prominently and the forward invitation at a smaller but readable size. The home message belongs in accompanying story copy and future insert copy; do not crowd every message onto every printable face. A candle emoji may accompany digital copy, but the supplied window mark is the reproducible merchandise symbol.

Use solid colors, full opacity and transparent backgrounds in print masters. The window represents illuminated welcome through contrast and warmth; it is not metallic ink, an electric light or a promise of glow-in-the-dark printing. Avoid seasonal decorations and gradients in small print. Supplier color reproduction may vary; screen colors are not a physical color proof.

## Typography

The exact raster masters use DejaVu Serif Bold for SOMETIMES / 57., DejaVu Serif Italic for the origin phrase, and DejaVu Sans for small branding and the forward invitation. These fonts provide a reproducible, openly licensed base. All text is rendered into the PNG; the production file does not depend on a remote font. Avoid substitutions, stretched type, extreme tracking and tiny thin lettering. Keep website typography inherited from the existing site design.

Minimum working standard: 9 pt for mug secondary copy at final printed size; 10 pt for printed inserts. The v2 mug closing line is 11.5 pt and LOLO branding 10.3 pt at 300 DPI. Tee small branding is 25.2 pt before any supplier scaling. Confirm final placement/scaling per SKU and size.

## Illuminated window

Use the existing simple four-pane gold window, with even panes and clear cross-shaped negative space. Keep it upright and unwarped. On garments and mugs, use the same solid gold. Clear space around the mark should be at least half one pane width. At small navigation sizes, use the icon alone or the wordmark without a tiny tagline. Do not add ornaments or replace the live LOLO logo as part of merchandise preparation.

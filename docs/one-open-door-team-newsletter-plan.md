# ONE OPEN DOOR TEAM — Newsletter launch checklist
Status: DRAFT — owner approved concept; not yet an active email subscription

## Intent
- Part of Leave One Light On, not a new company, paid membership, or store.
- Free public reading and participation; email subscription always optional.
- Newsletter name: One Open Door Team.
- Promise: "One open door can change a life."
- Editorial cadence: occasional, initially once or twice per month.
- Content: one authentic story (with consent), one practical act of compassion, brief organizational update when useful.
- No shame, aggressive appeals, hidden advertising, or automatic subscriptions from forms/contact requests.
- Don't publish identifiable private foster-care material or children's information without appropriate approval.

## Preferred provider
MailerLite Free plan (current as of Nov 2026): 250 subscribers, 2500 monthly sends, 3 forms. Limits and terms subject to change.
Owner must create/access a LOLO-controlled MailerLite account, authenticate sender and domain, and review provider contract/processing. Do not use personal/TJW marketing accounts.

## Activation sequence
1. Create LOLO mailing list named One Open Door Team using LOLO-controlled account.
2. Verify contact@leaveonelighton.org or another LOLO-controlled sender, and DKIM/SPF as required.
3. Create accessible embedded form with required email and **unchecked** explicit consent statement, e.g. "Yes, email me free One Open Door Team updates from Leave One Light On. I can unsubscribe at any time."
4. Enable double opt-in and verify confirmation email.
5. Add a real unsubscribe link and LOLO postal contact details as required by applicable email laws.
6. Update /privacy.html to specify newsletter provider, information collected, use, retention and deletion, third-party handling and unsubscribe.
7. Wire provider's exact verified HTML snippet; never invent form action or collect emails without endpoint.
8. Test subscription, confirmation, welcome, unsubscribe, error handling, and mobile/keyboard accessibility.
9. Once working, remove the explicit "email signup isn't active yet" notice, remove noindex from /one-open-door-team.html, link in site navigation or Get Involved page.
10. Owner approves publication. Preserve all existing site pages, forms, Light Board, story pages and Printful.

## Suggested initial issue (draft)
Subject: The first open door
Preheader: Someone made room. What if we did the same?
Title: One Open Door Team — Welcome
Body:
One open door can change a life.

Leave One Light On began with lived experience and grew into a simple invitation: notice the need, then take one meaningful step.

This is not a club you have to join to belong. It's a free place to share stories, celebrate ordinary compassion, and remember that sometimes the smallest act of welcome matters more than we know.

This month's small step: Think of someone you haven't checked on lately. Reach out. No grand speech is required.

Thank you for carrying a little light forward.

— Leave One Light On
Footer: Why you received this email, organization contact, and provider-managed unsubscribe (to be added and verified before sending).

## Guardrails
- Do not send to existing contacts who did not explicitly opt in.
- Do not claim 501(c)(3) federal recognition or tax-deductible gifts.
- Do not activate a paid plan without owner approval.
- Newsletter sponsorships, donations, and merchandise are separate optional actions.
- Do not merge or publish while the signup is not functional unless owner explicitly approves a read-only informational launch.

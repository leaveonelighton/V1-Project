Why it belongs here

Leave One Light On is built on practical compassion. Blood donation is one direct way an eligible person can serve a neighbor without recognition or fanfare.

What to expect

Eligibility, appointment length, and donation options vary. A qualified blood center should answer health and eligibility questions. Do not rely on this website for medical guidance.

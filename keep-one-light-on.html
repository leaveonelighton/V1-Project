A Movement of Hope

Leave One Light On

See. Care. Act. Repeat.

I was once the child searching for a light in the darkness. Someone left a light on for me. Now it is my turn to leave one on for someone else.

Jerry and Peg Gorman opened their home when they had every reason to close the door. Their example became the foundation of The Light in the Window and inspired the Leave One Light On movement.

This movement is not about perfection. It is about ordinary people choosing one achievable act of compassion at a time.

Broken people do not need perfect people. They need people who stay.

What can you offer today?

Five minutes

Let someone know they matter.

Send a thank-you. Reconnect without demanding a reply. Ask a neighbor how they’re doing.

Try this: “You came to mind today. How are you?” Then give them room to answer.

One hour

Offer something practical.

Listen without rushing. Help with a task. Share a skill. Connect someone to a useful existing resource.

Try this: “Would it help if I spent an hour on that with you?” Ask first and respect their answer.

You do not have to carry this moment alone.

Leave One Light On is not a crisis service, but these trusted services can help you find the right support.

Immediate danger

If you or someone else is in immediate danger in the United States, call 911 now.

Emotional distress or thoughts of suicide

Call or text 988, or use the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. It is available 24 hours a day.

Food, housing, utilities, or local services

Call 211 or visit 211.org to find local help.

Concern about child abuse or neglect

Visit the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline for call, text, or chat support.

Outside the United States: contact your local emergency number or local crisis service.

Reach the 988 Lifeline

This page points to independent services. Leave One Light On does not provide emergency, medical, legal, or mental-health care.

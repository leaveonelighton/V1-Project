988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential support from a trained crisis counselor. Call or text 988, or use online chat.

Veterans Crisis Line

Veterans, service members, and loved ones can dial 988, then press 1, or text 838255.

Visit Veterans Crisis Line →

Confidential safety planning and local support. Call 800-799-SAFE (7233) or text START to 88788.

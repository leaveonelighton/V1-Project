5 Cedar Hill Road

The real home at the center of the founding story.

A house that became a welcome

Cedar Hill Road matters because of what happened there: Peg and Jerry Gorman made room for young people who needed belonging, stability, and ordinary family life.

This page preserves the place without turning a private home into a spectacle. Historical photographs and architectural details should be published only when ownership, privacy, caption, and accessibility information are confirmed.

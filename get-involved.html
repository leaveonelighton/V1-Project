Causes Close to Ted’s Heart

A wider field of compassion.

Ted’s life has made him especially attentive to children and families, foster care and adoption, mentoring and education, veterans and first responders, serious illness, blood and organ donation, homelessness, trauma, second chances, neurodiversity, workforce opportunity, and faith expressed through service. These are not competing missions. They are different places where one person can notice a need and faithfully respond.

Children, foster families & adoption→Mentoring, education & neurodiversity→Technology skills & workforce opportunity→Blood donation & lifesaving health support→Organ, eye & tissue donation→Faith, churches & servant-hearted action→Children facing cancer and serious illness — St. Jude↗Veterans, first responders and their families — Tunnel to Towers↗Wounded veterans and military families — Wounded Warrior Project↗Homelessness, trauma, healing & second chances→
Independent organizations: St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital, Tunnel to Towers Foundation, and Wounded Warrior Project are causes Ted personally respects. They are independent organizations and are not represented here as sponsors, partners, or endorsers of Leave One Light On. Always review an organization’s current programs and giving information on its official website.

One open door can change a life.

You're welcome here

Someone showed up for us. Now we can show up for someone else. One Open Door Team shares stories of kindness received, simple ways to notice a need, and encouragement to make room for one more.

No membership. No fee. No purchase required. Everyone is welcome to read and participate in the movement, whether or not they ever subscribe to email updates.

What you'll find

  • Real stories and lessons about belonging, gratitude and compassion
  • One practical idea you can try in your everyday life
  • Occasional news from Leave One Light On, including community stories and events

Email updates: optional

We plan to offer a free, occasional email newsletter. When signup opens, it will require your explicit consent, confirmation of your email address, and an easy unsubscribe link in every issue. We will not enroll anyone automatically or turn contact messages into newsletter subscriptions.

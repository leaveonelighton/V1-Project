Transparency and accountability

Clear about who we are. Careful about what comes next.

Our organizational status

Leave One Light On is a Texas nonprofit corporation, incorporated August 14, 2026. Federal recognition under section 501(c)(3) has not been obtained. LOLO does not promise tax deductibility.

Governance and financial stewardship

The board adopted bylaws and approved financial controls and bank-authority documentation in October 2026. LOLO is committed to separate organizational accounting and board oversight. Banking and payment-provider details are verified through the organization's records, not this page.

Our stewardship commitments are to keep LOLO funds separate from personal funds and commercial business funds, document receipts and spending, and maintain board oversight of mission-related decisions.

These commitments describe the standards LOLO is preparing to operate under. They are not a published audit or a claim that banking, reconciliation and reporting procedures have all been independently verified.

Acknowledgments and records

A payment confirmation records the amount and date of a processed payment. It is not a statement that the payment is tax deductible. Monthly terms and cancellation instructions will be made clear before any recurring option becomes available.

Questions are welcome.

Ask about the mission, financial readiness or a contribution through our contact page. Please do not send payment-card details or banking documents through a message.

Keep the Light On

Gifts help sustain the educational work behind the movement. They are never the only way to participate.

What gifts support

  • Maintaining free, trusted resource guides
  • Research review, citations, and plain-language education
  • Website hosting, accessibility, and public communications
  • Discussion materials and community outreach
  • Responsible story preservation

Clarity before giving

Financial gifts are not being accepted through this website. No tax, fee, refund, or payment-processing claim is made.

If financial support is not the right step today, return to the invitation hub. Learning, mentoring, giving blood, supporting a family, and sharing a trusted resource all matter.

Keep the Light On

Gifts help sustain the educational work, research, free resources, accessibility, website hosting, public communication, and responsible preservation of the stories behind the movement. Financial support is always optional and is never the only way to participate.

Before you give

Financial gifts are completely optional. They are not purchases and do not provide goods or services in return.

Leave One Light On is not representing these gifts as tax-deductible charitable contributions. No tax-deduction claim is made.

Whether someone gives financially, shares a trusted resource, supports a family, gives blood, mentors someone, or simply leaves one light on for another person, they remain part of the movement.

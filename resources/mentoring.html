Frequently asked questions

Is Leave One Light On the provider of these services?

No. We organize educational information and link to external organizations. Contact the listed provider directly.

How do I find state or local rules?

Use the official state directory where available, then confirm information with the responsible state, tribal, county, school, court, health, or nonprofit office.

How are links selected?

Priority is given to government sources, federally supported projects, established national organizations, and clear program locators. A link is not a guarantee or endorsement of every local service.

One person. One meaningful step.

Compassion has somewhere to go.

Leave One Light On is a movement of hope and practical compassion. We plant seeds of kindness through stories, trustworthy learning resources, and everyday invitations to care.

Inspired by the teachings of Jesus, we welcome anyone willing to notice a need and respond with respect. Start with what you have. Let the other person choose what they welcome.

The welcome that became a movement

A home can change a life.

It started with room for one more.

It doesn't have to end there.

Fifty-seven lives. One open door.

Peg and Jerry Gorman opened their home at 5 Cedar Hill Road to fifty-seven young people over the years. Theodore “Ted” Johnson describes himself as their first official foster child.

We honor Peg and Jerry’s welcome and the individual lives connected with their home. Each person’s story remains their own to share.

The Light in the Window tells that founding story. Leave One Light On carries its invitation forward: notice someone, make room, take one meaningful step.

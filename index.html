No one is expected to do everything. Everyone can begin somewhere.

Time. Experience. Professional skills. Encouragement. Prayer. Knowledge. Generosity. A willingness to listen. A story. The question is not what everyone can do. The question is what has been entrusted to you.

Learn. Mentor. Support a family. Give blood. Explore organ, eye, or tissue donation. Use your professional skills. Pray. Share. Give financially. None of these is a measure of your worth. Each can be a meaningful expression of stewardship.

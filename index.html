A movement of hope

Sometimes the world does not need another loud voice.

Sometimes it just needs one light left on.

Leave One Light On is an invitation to practice hope, hospitality, and everyday compassion—one person, one home, and one faithful act at a time.

You are welcome here

Start wherever you are.

If you are searching, hurting, curious, or already following Jesus, there is room for you here. You do not need a perfect life, a large home, or extraordinary gifts to make someone feel seen.

The origin story

One house changed my life forever.

At thirteen years old, I arrived at 5 Cedar Hill Road looking for a place to belong. Peg and Jerry Gorman opened their door, their table, and their lives.

The Light in the Window tells the true story of ordinary people whose compassion changed generations—including mine.

The Light Keeper way

See. Serve. Steward. Strengthen. Share.

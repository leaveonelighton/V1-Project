Why fifty-seven matters

Two separate facts. One invitation to move forward.

Ted was the first of fifty-seven young people welcomed by Peg and Jerry Gorman. Separately, a federal benchmark reports approximately fifty-seven licensed foster homes for every one hundred children entering care.

These facts are not causally, prophetically, or statistically connected. The shared number gives the movement a responsible reason to learn, remember, and act.

“No act of compassion is ever too small.”

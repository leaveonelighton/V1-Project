Why we are here

Compassion has somewhere to go.

Leave One Light On is a movement of hope and practical compassion. It grows through ordinary acts of care, shared stories, and trustworthy resources.

Inspired by the teachings of Jesus, we welcome anyone willing to notice a need, ask with respect, and offer a meaningful next step.

One person. One need. One meaningful step.

How to take part

You don't have to do everything.
Commit to one.

01

Notice a need.

Look around. Someone may need encouragement, company, or practical help.

02

Ask first.

Listen with care. Respect their choices, including a no.

03

Take one step.

Make a call. Share a meal. Offer a ride. Do what you can.

04

Keep the light on.

Let kindness become something you practice, one day at a time.

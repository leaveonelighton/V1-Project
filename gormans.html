Peg and Jerry Gorman

Ordinary people who made room.

The Gormans welcomed Ted and, over time, fifty-six other young people into their home at 5 Cedar Hill Road.

Their legacy is not a claim that one family solved foster care. It is evidence that steady hospitality can give a young person safety, belonging, and a place to begin again.

Leave One Light On honors Peg and Jerry by inviting practical compassion without applause, competition, or pressure.

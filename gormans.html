SOMETIMES 57.

It started with room for one more.

The Gormans welcomed Ted and, over time, fifty-six other young people into their home at 5 Cedar Hill Road.

Peg and Jerry Gorman made room.

Theodore “Ted” Johnson was thirteen when he entered their home. He was the first of fifty-seven young people welcomed there over the years. His experience is part of The Light in the Window, the book that inspired Leave One Light On.

Every number has a name. The other young people’s stories belong to them; this invitation honors their dignity without speaking for them.

Their legacy is not a claim that one family solved foster care. It is evidence that steady hospitality can give a young person safety, belonging, and a place to begin again.

Leave One Light On honors Peg and Jerry by inviting practical compassion without applause, competition, or pressure.

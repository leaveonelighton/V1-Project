How to read the Living Pulse

Verified

Verified participation

A trusted partner, event record, or appropriate documentation confirms that participation occurred. It does not prove impact or guarantee an outcome.

Self-reported

Self-reported participation

An anonymous act shared by a participant and approved through moderation. It is received with dignity but is not independently verified.

Milestone

Movement milestone

A clearly labeled record of shared movement activity. It is kept separate from individual participation and is never treated as a measure of human worth or achievement.
